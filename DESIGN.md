# Design System: Azkar App

> Generated by `/impeccable document` (scan mode) from the incumbent implementation. Inferred prose is marked **Inferred**; do not promote inferred values to durable commitments without confirmation. Future work that needs a binding visual decision should consult the user rather than treating this file as locked.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Dawn Garden" (الحديقة الفجرية)**

The system is a **dawn-lit sanctuary of remembrance**. The product lives in the moment between night and morning prayer, when a Muslim reaches for their phone to recite adhkar before the day begins. The visual language translates that moment: emerald greens (`primary`) read as verdant growth and life; warm sand (`sand-50`) reads as early light on stone; deep midnight forest (`midnight-900`, `#022c22`) reads as the calm of late-night worship; amber (`accent`) is reserved for the streak — the fire of continuity. Cards are generously rounded so the eye lands softly; shadows are almost imperceptible in light mode, replaced by borders in dark mode; motion is spring-like on completion and gentle elsewhere. The Quranic text uses a calligraphic serif stack (`Amiri Quran` → `Scheherazade New` → `Amiri`), which sets the spiritual register above the Plus Jakarta Sans UI chrome that frames it.

The incumbent design is **mobile-first, RTL, calm, and emerald-rooted**. It is not a productivity app: there is no red error state, no aggressive motion, no dense data table, and no skeuomorphic ornament. The system treats the user's attention as sacred — every interaction is sized for one-handed phone use, every screen is a phone column (`max-w-md`, 28rem) centered on larger viewports, and every state change is a soft transition, not a hard cut.

**Key Characteristics**

- Mobile-first phone column (`max-w-md`) with a 6-theme engine (emerald default; blue, rose, amber, purple, cyan) and a dark mode that uses its own midnight-forest palette, not an inversion.
- One primary accent role with a 50–900 scale, defined as CSS custom properties so the theme engine can swap the entire scale at runtime.
- Three font families: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (UI), `Amiri` (Arabic display), and `Amiri Quran / Scheherazade New` (Quranic text).
- Very rounded corners (1.5rem–2.5rem on hero cards) that echo prayer beads and soft pebbles.
- Subtle shadows in light mode, hairline borders in dark mode — never both at once.
- Streak, completion, and celebration moments use **amber**, not primary, so the emerald accent is never diluted.
- Spring-like completion animations (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`) and Framer Motion page transitions; nothing snaps.

**Confirmed visual rejection.** Inferred from the absence of these patterns in code: the project is not Material Design (no elevation tokens, no ripple, no app-bar); not Cupertino (no SF Pro, no iOS-style chrome); not neumorphic (no inset shadows); not glassmorphic (backdrop-blur is used only on the bottom nav, sparingly). Do not import these conventions.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by **emerald** as the single primary accent and **warm sand** as the neutral surface, with **amber** reserved for streak and celebration, and **midnight forest** carrying the dark-mode identity. Six themes (`emerald`, `blue`, `rose`, `amber`, `purple`, `cyan`) re-bind the entire primary scale via CSS custom properties on `:root` and `[data-theme='…']`. All values below are the **emerald** defaults — other themes follow the same 50–900 structure with their canonical swatch.

### Primary

- **Verdant 50 — Quiet Wash** (`rgb(236 253 245)`): pill background behind the active bottom-nav item, the very-soft tint behind primary CTAs. Used at small areas only.
- **Verdant 500 — Emerald Accent** (`#10b981`): the canonical accent. Used for primary buttons, the active dot indicator on the nav, the on-air ring around a playing zikr, and the Tasbeeh counter fill when a session is active.
- **Verdant 600 — Pressed Primary** (`#059669`): the fill of the primary CTA (`bg-primary-600 hover:bg-primary-500`). Slightly darker than 500 so the resting state is brighter than the hover.
- **Verdant 700 — Active Nav Text** (`#047857`): the color of the active nav label and the playing-audio icon in light mode.
- **Verdant 900 — Deep Verdant** (`#064e3b`): the deep end of the scale, used sparingly in borders and on dark-mode primary surfaces.

### Secondary

- **Saffron Gold 500** (`#f59e0b`, `accent.DEFAULT`): reserved for the streak (`FireIcon`), the "تذكير" reminder badges, and the confetti palette. **It must never replace primary.** It is celebration, not navigation.
- **Saffron Light** (`#fcd34d`, `accent.light`): hover state for saffron.
- **Saffron Deep** (`#b45309`, `accent.dark`): pressed state for saffron.

### Tertiary

- **Inferred — omit.** The product has no third role beyond primary + accent. Do not invent a tertiary; if a third color is needed, it should be a dark-mode-specific midnight surface.

### Neutral

- **Warm Sand 50** (`#fafaf9`, `sand-50`): the body background in light mode — the dawn-lit stone.
- **Warm Sand 100** (`#f5f5f4`, `sand-100`): pill backgrounds, soft chips.
- **Warm Sand 200** (`#e7e5e4`, `sand-200`): dividers, button-stroke borders at rest.
- **Midnight Forest 900** (`#022c22`, `midnight-900`): the dark-mode surface for cards, the completion screen, and the focus-mode card. Pairs with a `midnight-800` border.
- **Midnight Deep 950** (`#011c16`, `midnight-950`): the body background in dark mode — the very deepest green-black.
- **Ink 800** (`#111827`, `midnight-800`): the Tailwind default used in the design system as a near-black surface; appears in dark-mode borders.

### Named Rules

- **The Emerald Stays Emerald Rule.** Even when the user picks a non-emerald theme, the brand identity does not move. The emerald default is the system's "home" color; other themes are expressions, not replacements.
- **The One Voice Rule.** `primary.500/600` is the only color that may carry a navigational or interactive weight on a screen. `accent` is for streak and celebration only. Status surfaces (success, error) are not yet defined — keep quiet rather than introduce a red.
- **The Quiet Wash Rule.** Light washes (`primary.50`, `primary-900/20`) appear only behind an active item or a hovered element — never as a full-bleed surface. The body is always `sand-50` (light) or `midnight-950` (dark).
- **The Time-of-Day Gradient Rule.** Time-of-day-reactive surfaces (Welcome Card evening wash, Quick-Access Tiles, the Smart Suggestion widget on Home, any future "it's night / evening / day" surface) must pick from exactly three gradient formulas. No ad-hoc gradients are allowed:
  - **Day** (`05:00–17:00` local): `from-emerald-500 to-emerald-700` — the dawn garden in full sun.
  - **Evening** (`17:00–21:00` local): `from-indigo-600 to-purple-700` — twilight cooling into night.
  - **Sleep** (`21:00–05:00` local): `from-midnight-900 to-indigo-600` — the deep, dark night, anchored on midnight forest rather than a flat black.
  All three are intentionally distinct so a returning user can read the moment from the gradient alone. The 96px primary circle on the Completion Card is the lone emerald accent that crosses all three — celebration outranks time of day. The current `from-indigo-600 to-blue-800` on `SmartSuggestionWidget` (sleep branch) is a known non-conforming instance that has been re-themed to `from-midnight-900 to-indigo-600`; no other indigo gradient is permitted outside this rule.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Amiri (with `ui-serif, Georgia, serif` fallback)
**Body Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (with `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif` fallback)
**Quranic Font:** Amiri Quran → Scheherazade New → Amiri (specialized Quranic serif stack)

**Character:** Three-font pairing in which a Western geometric sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) handles all UI chrome — controls, navigation, English translations — while two Arabic serifs carry the spiritual content. `Amiri` is used for greetings, Surah titles, and the loading wordmark (the word `أذكار`); `Amiri Quran` is reserved for Quranic ayahs, where the calligraphic alternates and ligatures matter. The sans-serif is heavy (weights 400, 500, 600, 700, 800 all in use) so UI chrome can read confidently at small sizes; the Arabic serifs lean on their own elegance and stay at large sizes.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (`Amiri 700`, 1.875rem–2rem, lh 1.1): `home_greeting` (`السلام عليكم`), loading wordmark (`أذكار`), completion headline. Where the page introduces itself.
- **Headline** (`Plus Jakarta Sans 800`, 1.25rem, lh tight): section titles, settings groups. Where the page labels itself.
- **Title** (`Plus Jakarta Sans 700`, 1rem, lh 1.5): card headers, list rows, modal titles. Where the page addresses a single element.
- **Body** (`Plus Jakarta Sans 500`, 0.875rem–1rem, lh 1.5): all translations, descriptions, settings copy. UI voice.
- **Label** (`Plus Jakarta Sans 700`, 0.625rem–0.75rem, tracking 0.05em, often uppercase): counter badges (`1 / 12`), nav labels (`10px font-bold`), `azkar_reference` (`text-[10px] font-bold tracking-wide uppercase`). Where the page counts and labels.
- **Quranic Body** (`Amiri Quran`, `var(--quran-fs, 1.75rem)`, lh 2.2): ayah text only. The font-size CSS variable lets the user scale Quran reading independently from the rest of the app.

### Named Rules

- **The Arabic-First Rule.** Arabic text is always set in `Amiri` or `Amiri Quran`. `Plus Jakarta Sans` is for UI chrome and English translations only — never for Arabic.
- **The Quranic Register Rule.** Quranic ayahs use `font-quran` (`Amiri Quran` → `Scheherazade New` → `Amiri`). Do not substitute Plus Jakarta Sans or any sans-serif for Quranic text.
- **The Heavy Sans Rule.** UI chrome uses 500 weight minimum (`font-medium`, `font-bold`, `font-extrabold`). 400 weight is reserved for body translations and never appears in chrome that needs to read at a glance.

## Layout

The layout system is **a single phone column centered on larger viewports**. Inside that column, every screen is a vertical scroll with a fixed top header (where applicable), a flexible content area, and a fixed bottom navigation bar on the five main tabs. RTL is hardcoded at `<html dir="rtl">` and never switches.

- **App shell** (`App.tsx`): outer `h-screen w-screen` with the theme + dark-mode class chain, an inner `max-w-md mx-auto` container, `Safe-area` aware padding (iOS notch and home indicator via `env(safe-area-inset-...)` and the `.pb-safe` / `.pt-safe` utilities in `index.css`).
- **Top bar** (44px, `p-4 shrink-0`): settings + search buttons on the left in LTR / right in RTL, page title centered. Solid background `bg-gray-50 dark:bg-[#12241C]`.
- **Bottom nav** (72px / `h-18`, `max-w-md mx-auto`, backdrop-blur-xl, hairline top border, `.pb-safe` for the home indicator): five evenly distributed `NavItem`s. Active item gets a pill background (`bg-primary-50 dark:bg-primary-900/20`), a `-translate-y-1` lift, and a 1px dot below.
- **Screen content** (`flex-grow overflow-y-auto px-4 pb-20`): 16px horizontal padding, 80px bottom padding so content clears the nav.
- **Section rhythm**: 24px (`space-y-6`) between major widgets, 12px (`gap-3`) between quick-access tiles, 24px (`mb-6`) between the welcome card and the next section.
- **Responsive behavior**: beyond `max-w-md` (≥448px), the column is centered with the surrounding area in the body color — there is no multi-pane layout, no side rail, no expanded navigation. **The product does not adapt to desktop.** Treat any request to "make it work on desktop" as a design system decision, not a CSS tweak.

## Elevation & Depth

The system uses **a tonal-layering strategy in dark mode and a hairline-shadow strategy in light mode** — never both at once on the same surface.

- **Light mode**: cards lift off `sand-50` with a soft, long, low-opacity shadow: `shadow-[0_10px_40px_-10px_rgba(0,0,0,0.05)]`. Hover deepens it slightly: `hover:shadow-[0_20px_40px_-10px_rgba(0,0,0,0.1)]`.
- **Dark mode**: shadows are disabled (`dark:shadow-none`); depth is conveyed with a hairline border (`border-midnight-800`) on a slightly lifted surface (`midnight-900` over `midnight-950`). The CompletionScreen uses `bg-midnight-900` (`#1A3129` in some variants) over a `bg-midnight-950` body to achieve the same effect at a larger scale.
- **Focus mode card** uses a stronger shadow at scale: `shadow-xl dark:shadow-none` plus a 1px ring (`ring-1 ring-primary-500`) when audio is playing.
- **No inner shadows, no neon glows, no drop-shadow on type.** The one permitted SVG filter is the logo glow in the LoadingFallback, which uses `feGaussianBlur` for a soft halo — this is the only place a glow appears.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Rest shadow** (`0 10px 40px -10px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`): default on zikr, ayah, welcome, and quick-access cards.
- **Hover shadow** (`0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`): paired with `active:scale-[0.98]` on the zikr card.
- **Focus card shadow** (`shadow-xl` → Tailwind default `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0,0,0,0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`): only on the focus-mode zikr card.
- **Primary CTA halo** (`shadow-lg shadow-primary-500/30`): a colored shadow under the completion check button and the streak counter, where the emerald glows outward from the surface.

### Named Rules

- **The Flat By Default Rule.** Surfaces are flat at rest. Shadow appears only as a response to state (hover, elevation, focus) or as a colored halo on a primary CTA. Never both.
- **The No Dark Shadow Rule.** In dark mode, never apply `shadow-*` to a card. Depth comes from the `midnight-900 over midnight-950` layering, with a hairline border.

## Shapes

The form language is **soft, generous, and consistently rounded**. Corners are not decorative — they echo prayer beads and the smooth surfaces of natural stone.

- **Hero cards** (welcome, completion, focus-mode zikr): `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-[2.5rem]` (24px–40px). The most prominent element on the screen gets the most generous radius.
- **Section cards** (zikr, ayah, settings row): `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` (16px–24px).
- **Quick-access tiles, nav-item pills, primary buttons**: `rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl` (12px–24px).
- **Chips, badges, streak indicators**: `rounded-full` (pill). The streak chip on the welcome card is `rounded-full` with `bg-orange-50 dark:bg-orange-900/20`.
- **Ayah end-marker**: a 36px octagonal star (an SVG 8-point star with a stroke and a soft fill) wraps the Arabic-Indic ayah number. This is the only shape that breaks the soft-corner rule, and it does so deliberately as a Quranic ornament.

Borders are 1px and always `border-white dark:border-midnight-800` (or themed equivalent). No double borders, no dashed borders, no thick borders. The active state on the zikr card is a 1px ring (`ring-1 ring-primary-500`) — not a heavier border.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- **Do** keep the primary accent role single-voiced. Use `primary.500/600` for navigation, primary CTAs, the playing-audio ring, the active nav pill, and the streak counter. **One voice per screen.**
- **Do** reserve `accent` (amber/gold) for streak, confetti, and celebration moments. It must never replace primary for navigation.
- **Do** render Arabic text in `Amiri` or `Amiri Quran`. Use `font-quran` for Quranic ayahs, `font-serif` for greetings and the wordmark, `font-sans` for UI chrome and English translations only.
- **Do** size tap targets for one-handed phone use (≥40px) and make the entire zikr card a tap target for the count increment.
- **Do** convey depth in dark mode with a 1px border on a slightly lifted surface (`midnight-900` over `midnight-950`); in light mode with the rest shadow. Never both at once.
- **Do** trigger haptic feedback (`HapticService.light()`) on tap and (`HapticService.success()`) on adhkar completion. Haptics are opt-in but are the default on.
- **Do** animate state transitions with Framer Motion: page = `x: 20 → 0` slide with `easeInOut` over 250ms; main-tab swap = opacity + 10px y over 200ms; card completion = spring overshoot (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`) over 800ms.

### Don't

- **Don't** introduce a third color role beyond primary + accent. Status colors (success/error) are deliberately undefined; introduce them only after a product decision, not as decoration.
- **Don't** apply Tailwind's default `shadow-md`, `shadow-lg`, `shadow-xl` to cards in dark mode. Dark mode depth comes from borders, not shadows.
- **Don't** add a backdrop-blur beyond the bottom nav. The product is not glassmorphic.
- **Don't** render Arabic text in `Plus Jakarta Sans` or any sans-serif. Arabic is always Amiri / Amiri Quran.
- **Don't** use `shadow-lg shadow-primary-500/30` outside completion, streak, and primary-CTA surfaces. Colored halos are reserved for moments of celebration, not chrome.
- **Don't** add sidebars, side rails, multi-pane layouts, or expand the column beyond `max-w-md`. The product is mobile-first; desktop is a centered phone column.
- **Don't** introduce English chrome labels ("Home", "Settings") even in dark-mode contexts. UI copy is Arabic; the bottom-nav English class names in JSX are not user-facing.
- **Don't** use `shadow-inner`, inset shadows, or neon glows. The system is not neumorphic and not cyber.
- **Don't** promote an inferred value from this file to a locked commitment. The North Star, the descriptive color names, and the elevation philosophy are inferred from the incumbent implementation; they are a hypothesis, not a verdict.
- **Don't** introduce an indigo gradient outside the **Time-of-Day Gradient Rule** (Colors → Named Rules). Indigo belongs to the evening and sleep gradient slots and to nothing else; ad-hoc indigo→blue or indigo→violet gradients on new surfaces are not permitted.
